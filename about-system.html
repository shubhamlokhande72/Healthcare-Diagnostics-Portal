About the System

Objectives

The primary objective of this Point-of-Care Technology (POCT) is to provide rapid, highly accurate, and standardized readings of urine dipsticks. By automating the visual inspection process, we aim to eliminate human error caused by variable lighting conditions and color blindness.

Working Principle

The system utilizes a miniature colorimeter. Light of a specific wavelength is shined onto the reagent pad of the test strip. The reflected light is measured by a photodiode, which calculates the exact RGB values. These values are then processed by our AI algorithm to determine the precise concentration of the analyte.

Benefits

This automated reader offers several advantages over manual visual reading: 1. Higher sensitivity and specificity.
2. Automated record keeping for patient history.
3. Instant report generation in remote or rural settings.
